# 66. Intergalactic Tinder

## 💡 Core Concept (Concept)
Swipe to match in a virtual celestial zone filled with cold silence and death. This application only allows you to search for people who have also installed this boring scanning chip in an area within a hundred light years. Cards are generated based on the weapon weight and fuel level of the opponent's spaceship. If both parties press "Match" (like), the system will automatically synthesize and drop a glowing romantic encounter time capsule prop with the same unique UUID number into the package for both of you.
