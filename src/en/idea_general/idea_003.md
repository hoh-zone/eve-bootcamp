# 3. Decentralized Space Pawnshop

## 💡 Core Concept (Concept)
Allows players to lock server-wide limited edition NFT paints or rare blueprints into smart storage boxes, and the contract will automatically issue high-liquidity SUI loans based on price feeds from the oracle. If the loan is not repaid upon maturity, it will be immediately publicly auctioned.
