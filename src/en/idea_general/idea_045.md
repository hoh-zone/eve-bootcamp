# 45. Intergalactic Radio Station

## 💡 Core Concept (Concept)
A simple on-chain text array queue, players can append a Spotify music link to the end of the array at a very small cost. In any spacecraft cockpit UI connected to this radio station’s smart component, everyone will hear the same song simultaneously in this cold space, just like deep space watch.
