# 42. Space Billboard Protocol

## 💡 Core Concept (Concept)
If you capture a space boulder at the busiest intersection in the server. You can mount a 32x32 pixel grid based on the on-chain Display standard on this stone. Rich players in all servers can spend a sky-high price SUI to buy the right to draw pixels for a day through auction to display their alliance's logo.
