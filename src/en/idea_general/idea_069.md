# 69. Reverse Toll Booth (Reverse Toll Booth)

## 💡 Core Concept (Concept)
It is completely inconsistent with common sense in economics and even belongs to the logic of rich women throwing away money. A weird stargate established by a retired legendary wealthy businessman who invested a large amount of money: as long as you dare to pass through it, it will not only charge you a dollar for tolls, but will also use smart contracts to give you a large amount of generous handling fees - provided you are willing to accept it. It will be blindly drawn from your trunk warehouse and extremely cruelly annihilated by the void to evaporate the same core rare material that you have worked so hard to dig up.
