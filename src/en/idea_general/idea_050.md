# 50. Vampiric Weaponry

## 💡 Core Concept (Concept)
This is not a barbaric artillery designed to completely defeat the enemy. The ray it emits will cleverly intercept a very precise amount of energy after the decimal point after hitting the enemy's armor. What’s even more amazing is that these energies transcend the limitations of space dimensions and can be precisely drip-fed and transferred into the remote private vault of the creator of the fire weapon in a manner similar to micro-airdrops.
