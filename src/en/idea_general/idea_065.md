# 65. Driving and dying: Deep space Uber outsourcing group (Space Uber for Ships)

## 💡 Core Concept (Concept)
Due to the long journey, many wealthy people do not want to spend their lives rushing on time. This resulted in an extremely irresponsible distributed idle fishing spaceship hosting relay application: you used an agreement to temporarily erase your authority over your beloved ship and left it there. At this time, an Agalia kid on the other side of the world who had nothing to do in the middle of the night picked up this temporary authorization and randomly helped you send it to the delivery location across a dangerous road of two or three hours. As for how it was scratched or blown up in the middle - you are responsible for the huge consequences.
