# 90. Live Streamer Bounty Overlay for live broadcast room barrage and real-time online battles with real gods in the game. Gold master rewards consecutive kills and assassination instructions.

## 💡 Core Concept (Concept)
This module system perfectly blurs everything, what is the intervention of the off-site audience, and what is the limit of the authority in the game, breaking the boundaries of the four-wall masterpiece: If this famous opinion leader anchor with millions of hits is currently opening up wasteland and being watched by hundreds of thousands of troops, he is currently live broadcasting adventure survival and is extremely arrogant and arrogant. Well-known opinion leader anchor! Then this interactive system full of fun, live-action, and ridicule will be an extreme carnival: not only does it allow all the idle onlookers to use the encrypted real currency in their hands to directly pay the coveted head of this arrogant boss on this panel at any time, and the money will definitely be automatically deducted and deposited into the bounty pot within three seconds to put a big price; as long as a passerby who is in this active world at this moment and happens to be nearby gives this arrogant guy the last shot and generates a real final report! There is no need to wait for this lucky passerby to become rich overnight and get the huge reward that everyone just posted in front of millions of people!
