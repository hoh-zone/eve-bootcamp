# 51. Decentralized Alliance Stock Market

## 💡 Core Concept (Concept)
The alliance is no longer an organizational form, but a registered listed company. All core production capabilities of the alliance (total daily mining volume) are open and transparent and cannot be faked. According to the alliance's future mining expansion plan, players can purchase the alliance's equity certificates (stock tokens) with real money off-site. After the alliance uses these infrastructure funds to expand the stargate, it will return dividend distributions with fixed profits!
