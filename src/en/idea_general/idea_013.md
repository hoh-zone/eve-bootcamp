# 13. Alliance loot distribution system (Automated Loot Distributor)

## 💡 Core Concept (Concept)
Link the storage boxes used to clean up the battlefield after the war to a dedicated ledger contract. The fleet commander only needs to throw a large amount of loot into it, and the smart contract will instantly divide it evenly and put it into the wallet addresses of all participating team members.
