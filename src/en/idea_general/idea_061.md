# 61. Twitch barrage real-time air defense alert (Turret Tourette's)

## 💡 Core Concept (Concept)
Deploy an extremely neurotic Stargate Defense Cannon. The Boolean logic judgment of its muzzle orientation and firing does not look at any game parameters at all. Instead, its external data monitoring port is connected to the Twitch hotspot live broadcast room of a well-known game anchor. The audience's crazy typing of "Fire" and "Left" will cause the turret to perform a clown-like crazy automatic shooting without any tactical meaning.
