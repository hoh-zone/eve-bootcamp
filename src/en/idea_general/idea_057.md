# 57. Planetary Election System

## 💡 Core Concept (Concept)
A vast and fertile solar system attracts countless workers due to its rich ore reserves. In order to control this chaotic area, everyone decided to invest high amounts of fuel to vote for a galaxy chief with the highest privileges. The galaxy chief has the power to change the tax rates of all infrastructure and ore mining that pass through the galaxy during the entire server-wide settlement period.
