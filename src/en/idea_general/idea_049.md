# 49. AI Poet Biography Generator (Generative Lore Library)

## 💡 Core Concept (Concept)
The completion of each super inter-galactic jump star gate standing in the void was built piece by piece by tens of millions of blue-collar workers. This set of components will use the multi-modal large language model (LLM) connected off-chain to capture its construction history record and automatically create a poetic epic about the "Blood and Slag Builder" within seconds and inscribe it indelibly deep in the public chain.
