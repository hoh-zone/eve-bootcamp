## 💡 Core Concept (Concept)
Abandon all idleness and the sloppy artificial command center that always makes mistakes and fishes, and even misses several pieces of rare large ore because it didn't pay attention to go to the toilet! In this system, the commander only needs to guide the country in the resource-intensive points of a vast star district. Draw a mouse circle to determine a box-shaped target and select the coordinates of a deep and concentrated large ore storage box hidden in a safe and extremely secret point as the only feeding point: these eighty large ore ships will then use an inhuman, restless, extremely cold and efficient program mechanical algorithm to carry out extreme speed mining and air transport to suck up the earth and perfectly automatically return the ore to the deep warehouse. The application of the industrial ultimate machine monster module!
