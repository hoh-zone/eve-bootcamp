# 80. Space Gacha with Real Punishments

## 💡 Core Concept (Concept)
It is the ultimate display of the obsession with lottery culture and its self-destructive tendencies. Not only are you investing all your savings that may bankrupt you at this moment, waiting for the results in this seemingly shiny treasure-drawing machine that lures you into the quagmire of gambling; but this machine will not only laugh at your greed, it has a physical punishment connection line. Once you just draw out a useless bronze fragment or a comforting garbage option like "Thank you for participating", it will actually instruct your main cannon to fire a shot directly into your energy bank, triggering a real pain feedback of self-inflicted bleeding warning, a bottomless pit trap machine.
