# 52. On-Chain Escape Room

## 💡 Core Concept (Concept)
A "comic cryptex" built with the heart of a tech geek. There are a row of storage boxes placed in order. You must interpret a strange on-chain log hidden in the previous storage box (or a small piece of code that is only meaningful in a specific Epoch) as a password, and give the answer to the next smart box to open it. Push forward layer by layer until you finally get the hidden prize.
