# 54. Fully transparent and non-financial reputation system (Reputation Score Graph)

## 💡 Core Concept (Concept)
There are too many intrigues in EVE. In order to prevent insiders from causing trouble in small teams, a network system that only calculates the social interaction dimension is established. If you successfully trade with others, you can click on a positive review similar to Taobao SBT to move forward. Good reviews are not worth a penny, but before he enters the important star gate, the system will light up a red light to warn all guards - this guy is a top villain with a reputation value of minus 50 points!
