# 55. Monument to the Fallen

## 💡 Core Concept (Concept)
This is no longer a useful or economically efficient object. It is an abandoned, lusterless, oversized scrapped stargate that is not connected to any energy pipelines. But anyone passing by here can just drop a symbolic coin (tiny Sui) into it, and it will light up and add a final line to the list engraved on the chain, with the names of heartbroken comrades who have had all their account data wiped out in this epic space confrontation and have completely left the game.
