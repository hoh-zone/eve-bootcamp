# 37. Off-chain high-speed state channel (State Channel Skirmishes)

## 💡 Core Concept (Concept)
Two groups of players risked their lives to engage in a dogfight. However, because the TPS on the chain was not smooth enough, they isolated and locked the spaceship asset pools of both parties and entered into an off-chain microsecond server duel. After the duel was completed, they only needed to enter the only result (who lives and who dies) into the on-chain settlement box.
