# 59. Player-Generated Quests

## 💡 Core Concept (Concept)
Put aside the traditional online game formula of beating ten wild boars and getting two white clothes. Introducing the bounty panel function defined by all idle players, you can issue extremely weird and complex tasks with parameters, "For example, help me pick up 5 red wreckage fragments + and survive for 2 hours to come back to me." Since the conditions are established based on off-chain monitor triggers and the acceptance calculation is completed through deposit intelligence, this means that all ordinary players directly become AI masters with unlimited content output!
