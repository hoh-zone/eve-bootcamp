# 43. Space Theocracy and Divine Punishment (Sectarian Religion Framework)

## 💡 Core Concept (Concept)
You can believe in the non-existent "God of the Void" in the game. The wealthy people sacrifice (deposit) a large amount of SUI coins to the "God of War" they believe in; the temple's contract intelligently determines the size of the total reservoir, and will impose a randomly occurring divine punishment (Debuff) on unlucky miners who believe in other weak beliefs.
