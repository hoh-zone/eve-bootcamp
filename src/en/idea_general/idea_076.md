# 76. Cult of the Floating Default Cube rendered with missing corners material

## 💡 Core Concept (Concept)
A completely empty, gray, white, purple and black Default model block that was created due to an accidental texture rendering error in a certain in-game engine. As a result, a whole system of absurd believers who believe in the "God of Imperfections and Bugs" in this sacred unknown realm has emerged in this name: not only do they regularly organize gatherings here to pray, but they also must destroy countless valuable assets or resources that have been hard-earned without thinking as fuel for pilgrimage in order to please and protect them from collapse at various system levels!
