# 71. Space Potato Relay Racing

## 💡 Core Concept (Concept)
Transform the original non-destructible underlying Move "Hot Potato", a purely logical poison without Drop ability, into a substantial visual prop. After the starting gun of the racing game is launched, if the hot potato stays in a certain driver's position for a long time without being thrown into the cargo hold of another friendly racing ship through high-frequency transfer, it will cause the entire flight system to fail to pass the inspection and the engine will stall or even burst! Use the lowest level of indescribable architecture to realize an extremely exciting death chase game!
