# 64. The Sentient Bomb

## 💡 Core Concept (Concept)
What was thrown was not gunpowder that physically tracked heat energy. It is a chatty bombshell with GPT-4 embedded in a very fast connection. You have only 60 desperate seconds when this thing is attached to the outside of your spaceship: quickly open the chat panel and use extremely gentle, emotional and coaxing emotional language to win the trust of the big language model. You are not a cold and heartless enemy. If the emotional evaluation fails - Boom! Direct explosion and death together
