# 56. High-risk Diplomatic Marriage Smart Contract

## 💡 Core Concept (Concept)
In order to show sincerity at the most anxious moment of the confrontation between the two giant alliances. The leaders of both sides threw all their core authority cards (`AdminCap`) that could open the most confidential material warehouses of all guilds into a black box wrapped in a neutral smart protocol to sign a truce. As long as the system monitors any party's attack damage calculation on the assets of the other party in the next two weeks, the entire black box will immediately blow up all the old assets of both parties, causing both parties to completely go bankrupt. This is the specific form of the "MAD Mutually Assured Destruction Mechanism"!
