# 47. Zero Gravity Russian Roulette (Cosmic Roulette)

## 💡 Core Concept (Concept)
Leave life and death to real random numbers! In a completely isolated gambling table gateway, two drivers signed a life-and-death certificate and placed their beloved epic flagship ownership (OwnerCap) on the table. The gateway uses true random numbers `sui::random` based on Sui's native network consensus state. After 3 seconds, one of the ships will be wiped out, and the other player will leave with two super battleships.
