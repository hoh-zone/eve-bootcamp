# 58. Galactic Treasure Hunt takes over the galaxy

## 💡 Core Concept (Concept)
In a server-wide super easter egg event, the developer personally spent his own money to hide an astonishing amount of encrypted SUI tokens in a mysterious tiny safety lock. But the extremely long decoding fragments of this safety lock are scattered among the scraps of logs generated by various turrets deployed by hundreds of different players in this game, and even on the number of an NPC fighter registered... It depends on who has the decoding mind like Spielberg's "Ready Player One" to connect the mysteries of the universe.
