# 5. Dynamic Toll Stargate

## 💡 Core Concept (Concept)
The algorithm calculates the charge based on the traffic through the stargate in the past hour. If a large number of fleets want to take shortcuts during a fierce battle, the tolls will rise exponentially. This will not only make money, but also play a role in limiting flow and strategic cutting.
