# 70. Schrödinger's Cargo Box

## 💡 Core Concept (Concept)
A transit storage container platform designed using the mafia model and deep, completely secretive and opaque encapsulation functions. You are fully expecting to throw a priceless legendary turret into the system, and then the next time you decrypt it and take it out from another node thousands of miles away, it will directly collapse due to a truly random rearrangement inside: you have a probability of getting back exactly the same thing... Or you may only open it and the system will spit out a bunch of useless metal powder with a sneer (and the really good turret has actually slipped into the secret warehouse reserved by the designer)
