# 40. Completely decentralized encrypted darknet orders (On-Chain Encrypted Orders)

## 💡 Core Concept (Concept)
Are all the orders from the fleet's top brass clear? Utilizes the Diffie-Hellman key exchange principle to cooperate with the on-chain public key (zkLogin) of the EVE character. Only a client with a specific officer identity and a temporary private key can unlock the meaningless garbled string of words on the main network that actually issued the "All Attack Sector A" command.
