# 75. A minimalist visual reconstruction of the disorienting abyss fear patch (The Existential Dread Mod)

## 💡 Core Concept (Concept)
This is not a simple extension, it strikes those high-end players who are used to stacking countless reports and carousing with money figures from a deep psychological dimension: it completely uses front-end filtering to forcibly strip and erase those flashy data progress slots that are full of security, and restores the panel to a state where nothing is known. You only feel that you are in the vast, endless darkness and loneliness. It is as terrifying as a black hole, absolutely depressing, and can even make you unable to tell where you are flying. Finally, you gradually lose all sense of direction to survive.
