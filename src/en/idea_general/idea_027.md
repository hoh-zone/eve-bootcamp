# 27. Hybrid signature asynchronous settlement architecture (Off-Chain Sig_Verify)

## 💡 Core Concept (Concept)
Most of the high-frequency interactions that do not involve asset transfer (such as movement and aiming) are issued temporary Ed25519 signatures through the off-chain server. After players accumulate hundreds of signatures, they are settled on the chain in a PTB (Programmable Transaction Block). Greatly break through TPS and Gas limits
