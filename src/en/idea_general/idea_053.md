# 53. Countdown to Survival: Space Lotteries - Tontines

## 💡 Core Concept (Concept)
This is a cruel game of pure mental coercion. Each of the 100 people who signed the contract pledged a large amount of starting money into the prize pool; as the space war and resource competition progressed, if the system (KillMail) captured that someone on the list was blown to pieces, that person would be immediately kicked out of the list and their funds would be confiscated. The only survivor who insists on surviving until 1 second before the end of the game without any death stains on the record will take away the astonishing wealth of the entire game.
