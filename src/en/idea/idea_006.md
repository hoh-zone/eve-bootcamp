# 6. Bounty Hunter Escrow

## 💡 Core Concept (Concept)
Fully automated assassin platform. The publisher places a bounty on the head of a specific player, and the contract automatically releases the bounty to the assassin after the kill is confirmed.

## 🧩Pain Points Solved
- **Black and White**: Traditional assassinations often occur in situations where "the person is not killed after the money is given" or "the person is killed but the money is not given".
- **Anonymity Requirement**: Both buyers and assassins want to keep their identities hidden on the chain.

## 🎮 Detailed gameplay and mechanics (Gameplay Mechanics)
1. **Killmail Oracle**: The contract is connected to the battle damage log evidence generated by the EVE game server.
2. **SUI Lock**: The bounty is escrowd by the contract. Once the `Victim_ID == Bounty_Target` logic is triggered.
3. **ZK-Login Withdrawal**: Assassins don’t even need a regular wallet to receive money. They can use ZK-Login to generate a new address using a temporary social account (such as Twitch), collect the bounty secretly, and completely cut off the social relationship chain.

## 🛠️ Sui core feature application (Sui Features)
- [x] zkLogin (secret withdrawal)
- [x] DeepBook (if you have revenge materials, you can directly convert them to market prices)

## 📐 Smart Contract Architecture Planning (Smart Contract Architecture)
