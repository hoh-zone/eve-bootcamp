# 90. Permanent War Memorial

## 💡 Core Concept (Concept)
Use the idea of ​​immutable objects to make "immutable monuments" instead of unreasonable invincible buildings. After the Alliance completes an epic expedition, defends an important system, or experiences a tragic battle, it can turn the battle summary, victim list, victory declaration, and commemorative images into a permanent monument. The monument itself cannot be rewritten, but commemorative activities, military academy tours, commemorative NFTs and reward walls can be derived around it.

## 🛠️ Sui core feature application (Sui Features)
- [x] Sponsored Transactions: Facilitate the alliance’s collective participation in monument casting
- [x] Sui Kiosk: Selling commemorative merchandise or tickets
- [x] Walrus: Save high-definition commemorative images and documents
- [x] Move core mechanism (Immutable): The text of the monument cannot be tampered with

## 📐 Smart Contract Architecture Planning (Smart Contract Architecture)

### Core Object
- `WarMemorial`: Monument body
- `MemorialPass`: Commemorative event ticket or commemorative NFT
- `TributeVault`: Reward and Memorial Fund

### Key functions
- `mint_memorial`: Cast Monument
- `append_tribute`: Add reward or eulogy index
- `buy_memorial_pass`: Purchase souvenirs
- `fund_veterans`: Appropriations from the Memorial Fund

## 💻 Frontend & Client interaction layer (Frontend & Client)
Create war memorial pages, timelines, maps and hero lists. In the game, you can conduct guided tours and interact with flowers around the monument.

## 💰 Economic and Business Model (Economic Model)
- Sale of commemorative peripherals
- Union Memorial Fund
- Teaching war history subscription
- Brand cooperation and event sponsorship
