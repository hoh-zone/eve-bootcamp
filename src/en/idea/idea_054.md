# 54. Time-limited dark-light rental black display cabinets

## 💡 Core Concept (Concept)
Put the spacecraft into the Kiosk and set "Rent" to lend it to others. When the lease period ends, not only will it be forcibly deprived of recycling no matter where in the universe, it will be forcibly locked into this large display cabinet again.
